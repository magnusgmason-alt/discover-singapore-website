About the guide

One island. Every corner.

Singapore is small enough to cross in an hour and dense enough to fill a month. Discover Singapore is a complete travel guide to the city: when to go, how to get around, where to stay, what to eat, what it costs, local rules and etiquette, and how to handle the heat and the rain.

The garden city

Grown from the sea. Planted like a garden.

Singapore has grown by roughly a quarter since independence, much of it land reclaimed from the sea. Then it planted that land so thickly that it now calls itself a city in nature. After dark the Supertrees at Gardens by the Bay light up for the nightly Garden Rhapsody show.
